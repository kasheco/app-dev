#  My Favorite Movie and Series

##*Five Feet Apart*

**Five Feet Apart** is one of my favorite movies.  
It tells the story of two teenagers, **Stella** and **Will**, who both suffer from *cystic fibrosis*.  
Because of their illness, they must stay **five feet apart**, even though they fall deeply in love.  

> “Can you love someone you can never touch?”  

**Cast:**
- *Haley Lu Richardson* as **Stella**
- *Cole Sprouse* as **Will**
